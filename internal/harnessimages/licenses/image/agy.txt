sablecourt-harness-agy

This image contains no vendor software. It holds a Debian bookworm base
(packages from the snapshot.debian.org archive of 2026-11-23, copyright
files under /usr/share/doc) and an empty directory /opt/harness-tool.

The Antigravity CLI is proprietary software of Google and is not in this image.
`sablectl harness install agy` downloads the pinned version from Google's own download host on your
machine, checks the archive SHA-512 and the binary SHA-256, and stores it in a separate volume that the
harness container mounts read-only at /opt/harness-tool.
`sablectl harness login agy` runs that step when the login volume does not
exist yet or the tool is missing, of another version or does not match its pin.

Source: https://github.com/shadewright-labs/sablecourt
